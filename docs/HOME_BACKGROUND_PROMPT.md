## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: background art for OrbitBridge satellite mission desktop game home screen, landscape 16:9, 1920x1080 or higher.
Primary request: cinematic realistic Earth seen from low orbit, crisp luminous electric blue atmospheric rim, curved Earth occupies the bottom right half, rich ocean blues, visible Central and North America with realistic greens, scattered white cloud systems and subtle golden city lights along the terminator. Horizon rises toward right from bottom left. Deep dark navy starfield fills top and left, sparse tiny white stars with a very subtle blue nebula, polished NASA-style space photography look. One small detailed silver observation satellite with blue solar panels in the upper right at about 82 percent x, 22 percent y; graceful faint cyan orbit trail sweeping behind it.
Composition: leave the entire left region from x=8 to 46 percent and y=15 to 72 percent very dark calm clear negative space for real UI overlaid later. Main earth limb around 65 percent height in center and 48 percent height at far right. No text anywhere, no title, no buttons, no logos, no NASA branding, no watermark, no UI frames. A high quality full bleed environment backdrop, not a UI mockup.
